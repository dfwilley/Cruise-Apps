1 Your port stop

Walking pace and safety margins

Walking times are worked out from straight-line distance multiplied by the detour factor, not from a street route. Tenders, shuttles, stairs, weather and crowds can all add time.

2 Choose a side quest

More quests nearby

Quests use real places from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Opening hours, access and work in boatyards change, so check on the day, stay out of work areas, and ask before photographing people. Not affiliated with Viking.

Discovery journal

Backup

Save the journal on this phone (photos included) as one file, or load a file saved earlier. Loading adds entries; it never removes any.

Shared trip journal

Two (or more) travelers each sign in with their own email. Everyone in the trip can add discoveries and photos, and sees everyone else's.

Sharing isn't switched on yet: it needs a free Supabase project set up by the app's owner (see SETUP.md in the Cruise-Apps repository). Your journal on this phone works either way.

On a computer you can also click the link in the email instead.
